Giardino Radicale

2014
Borgo San Nicola prison, Lecce
“Radical garden”

Inside the Borgo San Nicola prison in Lecce, in the men’s section, the GIARDINO RADICALE workshop was born. Supported by a group of designers and artists, the detainees explore, through participatory processes, new imaginaries for transforming the common spaces inside their section, designing and modifying them collectively.
