Aδερφια-Fratelli. I figli di Evaristo de Chirico

2009
Narrow-gauge railway between Milies and Volos, Pelion, Greece
“Brothers. The sons of Evaristo de Chirico”

Video performance, Volos-Milies, Greece, 19’40”.

At the end of the nineteenth century the engineer Evaristo de Chirico was commissioned to build the Milies-Volos railway to link the Gulf of Pagasae with Mount Pelion, “…a small narrow-gauge railway that connected that Argonautic port with the interior of Thessaly.” (A. Savinio). A video performance made on the tracks of Evaristo’s train, dedicated to the landscape of the childhood of two twentieth-century artist brothers, Alberto Savinio and Giorgio de Chirico.

A video directed by Matteo Fraterno.
Editing: Nico Angiuli, Martin Kaltner.
The artists in various roles: Sabina D’Angelosante, Roberto dell’Orco, Matteo Fraterno, Zoe Giabouldaki, Petros Lekapinos, Nadia Kalara, Martin Kaltner, Maurizio Papagni, Maria Anna Quaranta, Panaghiotis Samsarelos, Ruggero Savinio, Silvestro Sentiero, Spyros Tsalapatas, Adonis Volanakis, Mary Zygouri.
Promoted by: Principi Attivi – Regione Puglia, Italy; City of Milies, Artemidos, Agria, Greece; Isa and Giorgio de Chirico Foundation, Rome.
